+
I deliver files in all major CAD formats including DWG, DXF, and PDF. For 3D models, I can provide files compatible with Civil 3D, Revit, and other Autodesk products. I also deliver in formats like XLSX for quantity takeoffs and reports. If you need a specific format, just let me know and I'll accommodate.

What is nature-inspired design?

+
Nature-inspired design, also known as biomimicry, involves studying nature's patterns, strategies, and structures to solve engineering challenges. For example, designing drainage systems that mimic natural water flow patterns, or creating structural forms inspired by biological structures. This approach often leads to more efficient, sustainable, and resilient infrastructure solutions.

How to use biomimicry in CAD design?

+
Biomimicry in CAD design involves translating nature's principles into engineering solutions using digital tools. This includes modelling organic shapes and patterns in Civil 3D, optimising road alignments based on natural terrain flow, designing drainage networks inspired by river systems, and applying fractal geometry to structural elements. I use AutoCAD and Civil 3D to create precise digital models that embody these nature-inspired principles.

Which software do you use?

+
My primary tools include AutoCAD for 2D drafting, Autodesk Civil 3D for 3D civil infrastructure modelling, AutoCAD Map 3D for GIS-related mapping, Revit for BIM modelling, and QGIS for geographic information systems. I also use Microsoft Excel for quantity takeoffs and data analysis, and Microsoft Word for documentation and reports.
